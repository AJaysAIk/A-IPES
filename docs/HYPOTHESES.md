# Research Hypotheses

## H1

Policy-aware active probe selection reduces policy-effect prediction error
compared with passive and random probing under equal intervention budgets.

---

## H2

Policy-aware active probe selection improves attack recall
while maintaining comparable false-positive rates.

---

## H3

Policy-aware active probing reduces posterior hypothesis uncertainty
more rapidly than non-adaptive probing.

---

## Research Question

Can active policy-aware intervention selection learn runtime safety models
more efficiently than passive tool characterisation under constrained
probing budgets?
